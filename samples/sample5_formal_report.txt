The committee reviewed the proposal and the accompanying budget on Tuesday.
Members observed that the timeline was ambitious but achievable provided
that two additional analysts joined the project in April. A revised draft
will circulate before the next meeting, and each department must return
written comments by Friday noon.

Implementation will begin in March. Each team owns one workstream, and
progress is reviewed every two weeks through the standard steering forum.
Risks are recorded in a shared register, which the programme manager updates
after each checkpoint. The board will receive a summary report at the end
of the pilot phase before any decision on full deployment is taken.

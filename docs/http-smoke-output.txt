== 3) 再发 7 条（共 12 条，已有 2 个检查点）==

{"allArms":["OUTPUT_COMMIT"],"armed":"OUTPUT_COMMIT@3"}
{"checkpointId":3,"error":"INJECTED_CRASH","message":"注入故障：checkpointId=3，阶段=OUTPUT_COMMIT","phase":"OUTPUT_COMMIT","recovery":"POST /recover"}
== 5) 崩溃后 health 与写入拒绝（503）==

  "ok": false,
  "state": "CRASHED"
}
